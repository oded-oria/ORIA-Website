Accessibility statement

ORIA LEFKADA wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who rely on a screen reader, keyboard-only navigation, screen magnification, voice control or their own colour and text settings.

Standard we follow

This website has been designed and built to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page has a clear structure of headings and landmarks, and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Everything can be operated with a keyboard alone, and the item that has focus is always clearly outlined.
  • All photographs and images have a text description. Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and controls meet or exceed the required colour contrast of 4.5:1.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% and the pages work on screens as narrow as 320 pixels without sideways scrolling.
  • Animation is minimal and is switched off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion. Nothing flashes, plays automatically or has a time limit.
  • The photo gallery viewer works with a keyboard and screen reader: use Previous, Next and Close, the arrow keys, or Escape.
  • The “Accessibility” button at the top of every page offers larger text, high contrast, underlined links and the option to stop animations. Your choices are remembered on your device.
  • The website does not use cookies, tracking or third-party content.

Known limitations

  • The website is currently available in English only.
  • One gallery image is a computer-generated architectural visualisation rather than a photograph. It is labelled as such.

How the website was assessed

This statement was prepared on 19 September 2026. The website was assessed by self-evaluation, using automated testing with axe-core together with manual checks of keyboard operation, focus visibility, colour contrast, 200% text enlargement, 320-pixel reflow and reduced-motion behaviour.
